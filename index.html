Toxic Movie – Story, Posters, Cast, Release Updates & More

Toxic, subtitled A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups, is an upcoming Indian film starring Rocking Star Yash in the lead role. Directed by Geetu Mohandas and produced by KVN Productions and Monster Mind Creations, the film has generated significant excitement with its striking promotional posters and cinematic visuals. This page brings together all available information about Toxic, including official posters, confirmed cast details, and release updates.

About the Movie

Toxic is an upcoming cinematic venture featuring Yash (credited as "Rocking Star Yash") in what appears to be a large-scale, visually ambitious production. The promotional material describes it as "A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups," hinting at a mature, dark narrative. The film is directed by Geetu Mohandas, written by Yash & Geetu Mohandas, and produced by KVN Productions along with Monster Mind Creations.

Based on the released posters, the cast includes Nayanthara as Ganga, Tara Sutaria as Rebecca, and Kiara Advani as Nadia, alongside Yash who appears to play a character named Raya. The film features cinematography by Rajeev Ravi and music by Ravi Basrur.
